in observing pui . A plausible choice for cui would be:
cui = 1 + αrui
This way, we have some minimal confidence in pui for every user-item pair, but as we observe more evidence for positive preference, our confidence in pui = 1 increases accordingly. The rate of increase is controlled by the constant
α. In our experiments, setting α = 40 was found to produce
good results.
One alternative method that also worked well to us is setting
